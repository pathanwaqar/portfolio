01About

SYSTEMS, NOT PATCHWORK

When the standard stack stops, custom engineering begins.

Most platforms solve the common 80%. I work on the remaining 20%: custom logic, data movement, permissions, integrations and operational details that make a product useful in the real world.

My work sits between business requirements and technical delivery. I map the workflow, define boundaries, connect services, build the interface and backend logic, then carry the project through QA, deployment and support.

The result is software teams can understand, operate and extend—without depending on a fragile stack of workarounds.
